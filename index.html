CrowdIO explained

Live audiences,
actively involved.

CrowdIO builds real-time audience interaction systems for live events. We turn crowd motion into shared large-screen experiences for rooms with thousands of people.

What is CrowdIO?
CrowdIO creates real-time interactive systems that turn a live audience into part of a shared on-screen experience.
What experiences do you create?
Collaborative crowd games, live polling, participatory visual art, and custom audience interactions for large screens.
Do attendees need an app?
No. CrowdIO experiences are designed for immediate participation without an app download.
